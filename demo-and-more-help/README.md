# demo-and-more-help/

The two lines of the demo menu, written for people.

## "Why should an agency care about any of this?"

→ **`why-this-is-useful/`**

The idea behind the repo in seven short pages: why AI writes *fine* and why
fine is the problem, how taste splits into mechanical rules, voice and one
judgement call, the write-grade-rewrite loop, what it means for an agency
with many client voices, one of Alex's own posts before and after his
rewrite, and the honest limits of this snapshot. Start at its `INDEX.md`, or
pick line 1 of the assistant's menu.

## "I'm technical. How was the rubric actually built?"

→ **`how-this-was-made/`**

The method and the numbers: how the four files feed each other, how version 1
was induced from 34 graded posts and 782 blind judgments, the version 2
Best-of-N pass and its documented deviation, the grading view as a grader
prompt, the feedback notation, and the provenance rules. Start at its
`INDEX.md`, or pick line 2 of the assistant's menu.

## "Is it going to do something to my machine?"

No. There is no code in this repo: only markdown files and a licence. The
assistant reads them; nothing is installed and nothing runs.
