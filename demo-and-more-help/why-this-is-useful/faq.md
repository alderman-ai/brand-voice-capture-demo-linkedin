# Questions an agency owner asks

**Is this my taste or Alex's?**

Alex's. The rubric in this repo encodes how he writes his own LinkedIn
posts, and it says so: "ground truth is his judgment and his alone." The
method is the point, not his taste. The same steps would capture your house
voice, or a client's.

**Does it work for Czech posts?**

Not tested. Every post in this repo is in English. The mechanical floor
(line length, unicode bold, emoji rendering) doesn't depend on language. The
voice layer would need Czech examples. Alex's note on the CEE region: the
biggest dial is how many modern tech words stay in English, "and that's very
doable."

**Is it only for LinkedIn?**

This rubric is. Its rules are about the LinkedIn feed: the "see more" fold,
mobile versus desktop, what renders and what doesn't. Nothing in this repo
shows another channel.

**What would it take from my team?**

Time from whoever holds the taste: grades, critiques and, ideally, a few
full rewrites with notes. Postable needs very little (the starting notes
plus some minor refinements); Good as a floor needs more. The rewrites are
the bottleneck, and clients won't write them.

**Can it grade my posts, or write some?**

Not in this demo. It explains how the rubric was built and why, using Alex's
own graded posts as examples. The rubric is still early (see
[Honest limits](honest-limits.md)), and he doesn't put his name on its
verdicts about anyone else's writing. If you'd like this built for your own
voice, reply to the LinkedIn message that brought you here, or book 30
minutes: https://calendar.app.google/biAvGYqEqUtmtsNv9
