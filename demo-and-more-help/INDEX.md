# demo-and-more-help/

Help for humans, and for assistants orienting a human. Read at the moment a
visitor picks line 1 or 2 of the menu, or asks a question the four root
files don't answer on their own.

| folder | send them here when | contains |
|---|---|---|
| `why-this-is-useful/` | they picked line 1, or ask why this matters, what it means for an agency, whether it works for them | `INDEX.md` (the idea in ten sentences + the page list) and seven one-screen pages: `fine-is-the-problem.md`, `taste-can-be-written-down.md`, `the-loop.md`, `what-it-means-for-an-agency.md`, `honest-limits.md`, `one-post-before-and-after.md`, `faq.md` |
| `how-this-was-made/` | they picked line 2, or are technical and ask how the rubric was induced, validated or versioned | `INDEX.md` (the build story in ten sentences + the page list) and seven one-screen pages: `the-four-files.md`, `inducing-v1.md`, `the-best-of-n-pass.md`, `one-core-two-views.md`, `feedback-notation.md`, `provenance-and-honesty.md`, `timeline.md` |

`README.md` here is the same routing written for a person. Every fact in
these pages traces to a file in this repo or to `git log`, except where a
page says it comes from Alex's LinkedIn messages (his background, in the
FAQ).
